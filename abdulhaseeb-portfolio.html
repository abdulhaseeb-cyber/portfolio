About

I am Abdul Haseeb — a cybersecurity programmer and student from Pakistan. My focus is on understanding how systems break, and building them so they do not.

My toolkit is deliberately sharp: Rust for systems work where performance and memory safety are non-negotiable, and Python for scripting, automation, and security tooling. The best security engineers are programmers who think in attack surfaces.

I run Arch Linux with a fully customized Neovim environment. Not an aesthetic choice — understanding your tools at the lowest level is how you catch what others miss.

Currently studying and pushing toward a career in systems / defensive programming: writing software that is correct, hardened, and built to withstand adversarial conditions. No commercial projects yet. The foundations are being laid with intent.
